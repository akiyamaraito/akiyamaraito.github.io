aphelune studio — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

aphelune studio is a private, single-user tool that the owner of the YouTube channel aphelune runs on their own computer to upload and manage the channel's own videos. It is not offered to anyone else.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

  • Upload and update videos, thumbnails, captions and comments on the owner's channel (scopes youtube, youtube.force-ssl).
  • Read the owner's channel analytics (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, search terms, comments) (scope yt-analytics.readonly).

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's own videos and to show the owner statistics about them.
  • OAuth tokens and fetched statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Viewer comments are read only in aggregate for the owner's own analysis; commenter names are not stored.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Security → Third-party apps with account access. Deleting the local token file also stops all access.

Contact

Questions: contact the channel owner through the aphelune channel page.
